From Slave to Jedi Apprentice

Anakin Skywalker was born on Tatooine, a harsh desert planet ruled by crime lords and gangsters. He and his mother, Shmi, lived as slaves under the greedy Watto. Despite his young age, Anakin displayed remarkable mechanical skill and bravery. He built his own podracer and even a protocol droid named C-3PO. His natural connection to the Force made him fast, instinctive, and fearless. When Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn met Anakin, he saw in him the potential to change the galaxy.

Anakin’s freedom came after winning a podrace that earned him a chance to leave Tatooine behind. Though he said goodbye to his mother with tears, he carried with him the dream of returning one day to free her and all those enslaved. This deep emotional bond would later shape many of his decisions as both a Jedi and a man.
