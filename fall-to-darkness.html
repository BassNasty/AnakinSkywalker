The Tragic Fall of the Chosen One

Anakin’s fall to the dark side began with his fear of losing Padmé. Haunted by visions of her death, he became desperate to find a way to save her. Chancellor Palpatine, secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, preyed on that fear. He offered Anakin the forbidden knowledge to cheat death—if he embraced the dark side. Blinded by love and fear, Anakin turned against the Jedi Order, believing they had betrayed him and the Republic.

In a single night, the Republic fell and the Empire rose. Anakin, now Darth Vader, led a brutal assault on the Jedi Temple, destroying everything he once fought to protect. His transformation was complete when he battled his former master, Obi-Wan, on the fiery world of Mustafar—a duel that ended in agony, loss, and rebirth as a Sith Lord.
